Featured Destinations

Explore India's magnificent historical sites and architectural wonders

Rajasthan
Jaipur City Palace

A magnificent palace complex that blends Rajput and Mughal architecture in the heart of the Pink City.

  • Rajput-Mughal architecture
  • Museum with royal artifacts
  • Chandra Mahal and Mubarak Mahal
Rajasthan
Jaisalmer Fort

The Golden Fort rising from the Thar Desert sands, one of the few living forts in the world.

  • Golden sandstone architecture
  • Living fort with residents
  • Jain temples and palaces
Rajasthan
Udaipur Lake Palace

A stunning white marble palace floating on Lake Pichola, appearing to rise from the water.

  • White marble architecture
  • Lake Pichola location
  • Luxury hotel experience
Agra
Taj Mahal

An ivory-white marble mausoleum and one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

  • Mughal architecture masterpiece
  • Symmetrical garden design
  • Changing colors throughout day
Khajuraho
Khajuraho Temples

A group of Hindu and Jain temples famous for their nagara-style architecture and erotic sculptures.

  • Nagara-style architecture
  • Intricate stone carvings
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
Hampi
Hampi Ruins

The magnificent ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire set amidst boulder-strewn landscape.

  • Vijayanagara architecture
  • Stone chariot and temples
  • Bouldering landscape
Kolkata
Victoria Memorial

A large marble building dedicated to Queen Victoria, representing British colonial architecture.

  • Indo-Saracenic architecture
  • Museum and art gallery
  • Lush gardens and water bodies
