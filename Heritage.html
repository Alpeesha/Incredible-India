8 Sacred Destinations

Immerse yourself in India's rich spiritual heritage through VR experiences that bring ancient temples, sacred rituals, and profound cultural traditions to life.

Featured Destinations

Explore India's most sacred spiritual destinations and cultural heritage sites

Varanasi
Ganga Ghats

The spiritual capital of India, known for its sacred Ganga river ghats, ancient temples, and spiritual rituals.

  • Sacred Ganga Aarti
  • Ancient temples
  • Spiritual ceremonies
Rishikesh
Lakshman Jhula

The yoga capital of the world, famous for its suspension bridges, ashrams, and spiritual retreats.

  • Yoga and meditation
  • Suspension bridges
  • Ashrams and retreats
Haridwar
Har Ki Pauri

One of the seven holiest places in Hinduism, known for the Ganga Aarti and Kumbh Mela festival.

  • Ganga Aarti ceremony
  • Kumbh Mela site
  • Ancient temples
Amritsar
Golden Temple

The holiest Gurdwara of Sikhism, known for its stunning golden architecture and community kitchen.

  • Golden architecture
  • Langar (community kitchen)
  • Spiritual harmony
Mathura
Vrindavan Temples

The birthplace of Lord Krishna, filled with ancient temples and spiritual significance.

  • Krishna Janmashtami
  • Ancient temples
  • Spiritual festivals
Ajmer
Dargah Sharif

One of the most important Sufi shrines in the world, attracting pilgrims of all faiths.

  • Sufi shrine
  • Interfaith harmony
  • Qawwali music
Ujjain
Mahakaleshwar

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, this ancient temple is a major pilgrimage site for Shiva devotees.

  • Jyotirlinga temple
  • Bhasma Aarti
  • Kumbh Mela site
Mahabalipuram
Shore Temple

A UNESCO World Heritage site with ancient rock-cut temples by the Bay of Bengal.

  • UNESCO Heritage site
  • Rock-cut architecture
  • Coastal temple complex
